Privacy controls

Delete your data.

LePan stores your files on the node you choose. Local device data and node data have separate controls.

Request help or deletion

Email cfa532@yahoo.com with the subject “LePan data deletion request”. Describe whether your request concerns support correspondence, local device data, or a node identity and stored files.

Do not include passwords, private keys, identity files, invitations or personal documents. We may ask for information needed to verify a request before acting. We cannot access or erase an independently operated node; its administrator must handle deletion there.

1. Stop backups

In Settings → Auto sync, turn off photo/video and selected-folder syncing. Use Stop sync if work is active. This stops future backup activity; it does not remove existing copies.

2. Remove node files and backups

Delete the intended items from Files or Synced. Open the relevant Trash and permanently delete the items when you are certain. Ask the node administrator about replicas, snapshots and retention rules. Removing an item from one device or node cannot erase copies already held by independent recipients.

3. Remove your device identity

In Settings, choose Forget this device. This removes the saved identity and associated local app data and stops backups. It is not deletion of the node’s user identity or stored files. To revoke other devices or remove a node identity and its associated records, contact the node administrator.

4. Clear remaining local copies

Android’s app-storage controls or uninstalling LePan remove its private app data. Files you exported to Downloads, Pictures, Movies or another app remain until you delete them separately.

Retention after a request

Support records controlled by the developer can be deleted on a verified request, except where retention is necessary for legal or security obligations. Node retention is controlled by its operator. See the privacy policy for details.
