Help and support

Let’s get you connected.

For LePan help, contact cfa532@yahoo.com.

Include your iOS or Android version, LePan app version, what happened, and the steps leading to it. Remove personal details from screenshots. Never send private keys, passwords, identity files or invitation secrets by email.

Connect to your storage

  1. Ask the administrator of your compatible Leither node for a device invitation or supported identity.
  2. Open LePan and import the invitation or paste it into the setup screen.
  3. Follow any device-approval steps provided by the node administrator.

LePan does not include a hosted storage account. The developer cannot restore access to an independently operated node or recover a lost private identity.

Photos and file backups

Open Settings → Auto sync. Choose photo and video access or select the folders you want to copy. Automatic backup is optional. iOS background availability, Android and device battery settings may delay background jobs; open LePan or use Sync now to continue. Stopping sync keeps copies already saved.

Node unavailable

Check your network connection and that your node is running. Ask its administrator to check its address, connection settings and your device permission. Do not post node credentials or identity files in a public issue.

Privacy and deletion

Read the privacy policy or follow the data and identity deletion instructions.
